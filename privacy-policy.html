This Privacy Policy explains how the game 3D Village: Robot Tractor (the “Game”), provided by [YOUR NAME / STUDIO NAME] (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play it. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information from you. In particular, the Game:

  • does not require registration, sign-in or an account;
  • does not collect your name, email address, phone number, address or any contact information;
  • does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • does not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools, and shows no advertisements;
  • does not send your gameplay data to any server owned by us.

2. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the Game saves a small amount of data only on your device (in the app’s or browser’s local storage). We never receive it.

WhatWhy
Game progress – coins and daily-reward statusSo your rewards are remembered next time.
Settings – tractor tyre choices and steering modeSo you do not have to set them every time.
Cached game files – 3D models, images and code librariesSo the Game loads faster and uses less data after the first start.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the app’s storage/data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Device motion sensor

If you choose the “Tilt Phone” steering option, the Game reads your device’s motion sensor (accelerometer) to steer the tractor. This reading is used only on your device, only while you drive, and is never stored, recorded or sent anywhere. On some devices the system may ask your permission first; you can decline and use the other steering options.

4. Third-party services and network requests

When the Game starts for the first time, it downloads some files (code libraries, 3D models and images) from third-party hosting services. Like any internet request, this lets those providers technically see standard connection information such as your IP address and device/browser type. We do not receive or control this information. The services used are:

  • Cloudflare cdnjs – cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • jsDelivr – jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net
  • Cloudinary – cloudinary.com/privacy
  • three.js project (threejs.org) and Polyfork (polyfork.dev) – hosts of 3D model files

Once files are cached on your device, the Game may not need to contact them again.

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to sell, rent, trade or share with anyone.
